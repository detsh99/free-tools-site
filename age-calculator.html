How is age calculated?

Age is calculated from your date of birth to today (or a chosen date). We count full years, then remaining months, then remaining days — the same way you'd count on a calendar.

Why do total weeks and hours look so large?

These are cumulative totals from birth. A 30-year-old has lived roughly 1,565 weeks or 263,000 hours — which puts the passage of time in a new perspective!
