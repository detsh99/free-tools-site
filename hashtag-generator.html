How many hashtags should I use?

Instagram: 5–15 targeted hashtags work best (max 30). TikTok: 3–5 relevant tags. Twitter/X: 1–2 tags per tweet. YouTube: 3–5 tags in description.

Mix big and niche hashtags

Use a combination of large hashtags (1M+ posts) for reach and smaller niche ones (10K–100K) where you can rank. This balances visibility and competition.
