Why Use a Strong Password?

Weak passwords are the #1 cause of account breaches. A strong password should be at least 16 characters, random, and unique for every site.

Our generator creates passwords entirely in your browser — nothing is ever sent to a server.

Password Security Tips

  • Use a different password for every account
  • Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) where possible
  • Store passwords in a trusted password manager
  • Never share your password via email or chat
  • Change passwords immediately if a site is breached
