What is a word counter?

A word counter is a tool that counts the number of words, characters, and sentences in a piece of text. It's useful for essays, social media posts, SEO content, and more.

Common word limits

Twitter/X: 280 characters · Instagram caption: 2,200 characters · Instagram bio: 150 characters · LinkedIn post: 3,000 characters · SMS: 160 characters.

Reading time estimate

The average adult reads about 200-250 words per minute. Our reading time estimate is based on 200 words per minute.
