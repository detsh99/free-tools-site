What can I encode in a QR code?

QR codes can store URLs, plain text, email addresses, phone numbers, Wi-Fi credentials, and more. They're readable by any smartphone camera.

What is Error Correction?

Higher error correction means the QR code can still be scanned even if part of it is damaged or obscured. Use "High" if you plan to print it on a surface that might get worn.

Tips for best results

Keep good contrast between dark and light colors. Avoid light dark colors or dark light colors. For printed QR codes, use at least 256×256 size.
