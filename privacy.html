Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

🔒 Short version: We don't collect, store, or sell your personal data. All tool processing happens locally in your browser.

1. Who We Are

DetshTool (detsh-tool.vercel.app) is a free collection of online utility tools. We are not a company; this is an independent project.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, or any personal details
  • We do not track what you generate (passwords, QR codes, UUIDs, etc.)
  • We do not store any text you type into our tools
  • We do not use cookies for tracking
  • We do not sell or share data with third parties

3. How Our Tools Work

All DetshTool utilities run entirely in your browser using JavaScript. No data is transmitted to any server. When you generate a password or create a QR code, that operation happens on your device only.

4. Hosting & Infrastructure

This site is hosted on Vercel. Vercel may collect standard server logs (IP address, page requests) as part of normal hosting operations. Please refer to Vercel's Privacy Policy for details on their data practices.

5. Google Analytics / Ads

We currently do not use Google Analytics. In the future, we may add Google AdSense to support the site. If we do, Google's advertising policies will apply and this policy will be updated accordingly.

6. Third-Party Links

Our site may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

7. Children's Privacy

DetshTool does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Our tools are general-purpose utilities suitable for all ages.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please visit our contact page.
