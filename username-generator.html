Tips for a great username

Keep it short (under 20 characters), easy to remember, and unique. Avoid personal info like your full name or birthday. A good username works across multiple platforms.

Which platforms have username restrictions?

Instagram & Twitter: 30 chars, letters/numbers/underscores/dots. TikTok: 24 chars. Steam: 32 chars. Roblox: no spaces. Always check availability on the platform you need.
