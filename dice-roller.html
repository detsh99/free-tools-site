Online Dice Roller

Roll any type of dice for tabletop RPGs, board games, or decisions. Choose D4 to D100 or set a custom number of sides. Roll multiple dice at once.

Dice Type

Common dice for RPGs

D&D and Pathfinder use D4 (damage), D6 (common damage/attributes), D8 (hit dice), D10 (percentile), D12 (large weapons), and D20 (attack rolls, saving throws).

Press Space to roll

You can press the spacebar to quickly re-roll the same dice combination without clicking the button.
